# See You Later? (Name is work in progress) We all need to go out more?

## What and Why?

In between text messages with my friends, there are hundreds of links scattered that lead to places we are dying to go to. There is no limit to the scope of where these places are, it could be a walk down the block or as far as the little island of Tuvalu. The point is, even after sending each other all these suggestions, we would always end up at our regular spot, completely forgetting about this place we wanted to go to and later find ourselves complaining that we always do the same thing everytime.

Planning trips is even more incoherent. We would have reels saved from months ago about the thrift store in NYC that we mutually agreed was a must-go when we landed, but it's week three of classes and we've barely left Manhattan. Not to mention, it's clearly way too many taps on our phone to open Google Maps, type the name of the place, create a pin and save it when we are in the mood of doomscrolling to oblivion. 

Thus, this app is born to make life easier for all of us. When you see a delicious cheap-eat restaurant, instead of letting this desire to visit disappear among your text messages, paste the link directly into our app (three taps total) and the rest will be done by us.

## For Whom?

The people who send each other way too many reels of places to go, yet become listless when free time to go there comes. And college students who are way too disorganized about their days, not knowing what's the plan on a Friday night.

## How?

The workflow (as of right now) that I'm proposing would be:

You find a place you want to go to, on say Tiktok -> our app will find the place using the caption (not guaranteed to work, thinking of workaround) -> drop the pin onto the map, also on a list for the "general area" (example: if you drop IPPUDO NYC, will go into NYC list, but say you have a restaurant in Abu Dhabi, will go into Abu Dhabi list) -> list is shared between invited friends.

### Product Backlog

The features that I'm currently thinking about including are as follows.

- This is a social app, so there will be profiles for you and your friends.
- You have the ability to create lists of places you want to go (example: NYC, Shanghai, Abu Dhabi) and invite the people you want invited into the list.
- Automatic pin dropping on the map based on the link sent.
- Ability to see "most-pinned places" or places your friends have saved to want to visit.
- Also ability for friends in your list to vote on whether the place is a "must-visit" or they don't want to go (additionally, add notes so they can explain).

### Don't want in app

- Reviews for places - too cluttered and I think linking back to GMaps itself should handle this.

### Possible future inclusions

- Recommendation system, get recommended similar places based on your pins (also consider travel style)
